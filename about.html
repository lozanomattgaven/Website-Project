ABOUT QUEUE

Music that flows, not just follows.

Queue is a music playlist-building platform designed specifically just for building a playlist that has the same vibe
with each song leading naturally into the next one. This is not a streaming service, and we arent just a shuffle button either
We are a tool for lazy people that cant be bothered to improved their own music taste to come in and make a playlist either
just for them or for everyone. Simple really.

How Queue Recommends

With Queue we have designed the perfect machine(Probably AI who knows havent implemented it yet) that takes a song of your choosing
and uses it as an anchor that recommends the next song after your first is done playing

STEP 01

STEP 02

STEP 03

User Preferences

STEP 04

Why Queue Is Different

Traditional music managers treat your library as a rigid spreadsheet file. Sorting options are limited to simple genre groupings or basic metadata. Queue looks at your music through the lens of continuous energy and emotional connection.
